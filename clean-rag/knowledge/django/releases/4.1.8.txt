==========================
Django 4.1.8 release notes
==========================

*April 5, 2023*

Django 4.1.8 fixes a bug in 4.1.7.

Bugfixes
========

* Fixed a bug in Django 4.1 that caused invalidation of sessions when rotating
  secret keys with ``SECRET_KEY_FALLBACKS`` (:ticket:`34384`).
