==========================
Django 3.2.7 release notes
==========================

*September 1, 2021*

Django 3.2.7 fixes a bug in 3.2.6.

Bugfixes
========

* Fixed a regression in Django 3.2 that caused the incorrect offset extraction
  from fixed offset timezones (:ticket:`32992`).
