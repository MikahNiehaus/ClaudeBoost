===========================
Django 2.2.14 release notes
===========================

*July 1, 2020*

Django 2.2.14 fixes a bug in 2.2.13.

Bugfixes
========

* Fixed messages of ``InvalidCacheKey`` exceptions and ``CacheKeyWarning``
  warnings raised by cache key validation (:ticket:`31654`).
